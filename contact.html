Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are your customer support hours?

Our customer support team is available 24/7 for urgent technical issues. For general inquiries, our business hours are Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM EAT, and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM EAT.

How quickly can I expect a response to my inquiry?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. Sales inquiries typically receive a response within 4-6 hours, while technical support requests are prioritized based on severity.

Do you offer customized solutions for large hotel chains?

Yes, we offer enterprise-level customized solutions for hotel chains and large hospitality groups. Our team will work with you to understand your specific needs and develop a tailored implementation plan.

What regions in Africa do you currently serve?

We currently serve clients in 15 African countries across East, West, and Southern Africa. Our regional offices in Kenya, Nigeria, and South Africa allow us to provide localized support and understand regional market dynamics.
