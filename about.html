Transforming Africa's Hospitality Industry

HospiTour was founded with a vision to empower African hospitality businesses with technology solutions tailored to their unique needs and challenges.

Our Mission & Vision

We believe that Africa's hospitality industry has immense potential to become a global leader in tourism and service excellence.

Our Mission

To provide innovative, accessible technology solutions that empower African hospitality businesses to optimize operations, enhance guest experiences, and drive sustainable growth.

Our Vision

To be the leading technology partner for Africa's hospitality industry, enabling businesses across the continent to compete on a global scale while preserving their unique cultural identities.

Our Story

From a simple idea to a pan-African hospitality technology leader

2018

The Beginning

Founded by hospitality veterans who saw the technology gap in African hotels and restaurants. Started with a simple booking management system for small hotels in Kenya.

2019

First Major Client

Signed our first major hotel chain client, expanding our operations to Tanzania and Uganda. Developed our comprehensive operations management module.

2020

Pandemic Response

Adapted our platform to help hospitality businesses navigate COVID-19 challenges. Launched contactless check-in and enhanced hygiene management features.

2021

Series A Funding

Secured $5M in Series A funding to expand across West Africa. Opened offices in Nigeria and Ghana, growing our team to 50+ professionals.

2022

Product Expansion

Launched our staff management and marketing automation modules. Reached 500+ business clients across 12 African countries.

2023

Award Recognition

Received the "Best Hospitality Tech Solution" award at Africa Tech Awards. Expanded to Southern Africa with offices in South Africa.

2024

Current Focus

Developing AI-powered analytics and expanding our integration ecosystem. On track to serve 1,500+ businesses by end of year.
